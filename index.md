# Sentence X-Ray Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Sentence X-Ray is a Chrome extension that shows the grammatical structure of an English sentence you highlight on a webpage.

## Summary

Sentence X-Ray does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## What happens to the text you highlight

- When you choose **Analyze sentence**, the highlighted text is analyzed entirely on your own computer.
- The text is never sent to any server. The extension makes no network requests.
- The text is not saved. When you close the overlay, it is gone.

## What we do not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No cookies
- No ads
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone

## Permissions

Sentence X-Ray asks Chrome for these permissions only:

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| `contextMenus` | To add **Analyze sentence** to the right-click menu |
| `activeTab` | To read the highlighted text, only on the tab where you clicked **Analyze sentence** |
| `scripting` | To show the analysis overlay on that tab |
| `notifications` | To tell you when a page doesn't allow extensions (for example, chrome:// pages) |

The extension cannot see any page until you click **Analyze sentence** on it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
